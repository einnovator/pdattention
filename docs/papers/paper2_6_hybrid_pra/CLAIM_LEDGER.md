## Explicitly unperformed evidence

- Original-order, randomized-location, and deduplicated-source Gate-B reruns have not been performed.
- A production retrieval engine or optimized lexical-index comparison has not been performed.
- Indexed parity has not been established for arbitrary corpora or low-overlap natural queries.
- Larger models, independent task samples, an effective deployable representation selector, and PRA-aware consumer training remain future work.

## Derived-audit method

`paired_retrieval_effects.csv` joins `end_to_end_rows.csv` by model, dataset, and example identity, computes per-question evidence-recall differences, and applies the manuscript's 2,000-draw paired bootstrap convention. The winning and best-fixed channels are held-out descriptive choices; the adaptive comparator is the fixed channel selected on validation (BM25 for HotpotQA, exact for QASPER).

`source_construction_audit.csv` reloads the frozen held-out identities with seed 20260823, applies `_bounded_source` with the pinned Qwen and SmolLM tokenizers, and evaluates the first four 32-token chunks using `evidence_token_spans`. Literal duplication counts evidence strings occurring more than once after tokenizer-specific truncation and decode. This audit performs tokenization and bookkeeping only; it does not run either language model.
