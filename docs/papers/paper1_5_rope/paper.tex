\begin{abstract}
Retrieved native-key/value (K/V) memory reuses attention states that were encoded outside the
current forward pass. This raises two questions that ordinary prefix caching can avoid: which
source position does each cached key represent, and which causal context shaped it? We give
Progressive Retrieval Attention (PRA) an explicit positional contract while preserving learned
absolute position embeddings as the default and adding rotary position embedding (RoPE) to
direct and retrieved-memory attention. In five-seed controlled tiny and small models, a compact
logical start offset per contiguous chunk removes first-layer position-reset error exactly for
both positional families. It does not remove deeper contextualization error; 50\% overlap helps
three of four tier--position combinations but slightly worsens the small absolute mean. A
four-cell pre/post-RoPE storage matrix establishes exact output parity when effective positions
match and shows that K-only rebinding intentionally changes attention. Deferred rotation is
therefore a flexibility mechanism, not an accuracy improvement, and raises the unfused warm
CUDA path from 0.179 to 0.649 ms in the measured small configuration. A continuously advancing
implicit-head experiment keeps every model operation at or below 24 tokens while the logical
source spans 192 tokens. Logical offsets and overlap often lower answer loss, but a constructed
single-chunk oracle is not uniformly better for tiny models, exposing a separate selection and
context-composition problem. These results separate positional identity, contextual continuity,
and retrieval quality. They validate the transport contract, not production long-context
quality.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A transformer key is not only a content representation. Depending on the positional mechanism,
it carries or interacts with a coordinate. This is easy to ignore while every key is produced
in one dense forward pass. It becomes operational when a system encodes a long source in
bounded blocks, stores native per-layer K/V, retrieves a sparse subset, and combines it with a
later query. A source key may retain its document coordinate, restart at zero in each block, or
receive a new coordinate when retrieved. These choices are not equivalent.

PRA provides a controlled setting for studying the problem. It stores full layer-native K/V
behind inexpensive routing gists and inserts selected states before direct K/V under one
softmax. Paper 1 established native transport, exact routing, model-bounded encoding, and an
implicit prompt-head reference. The present paper holds those mechanisms fixed and changes
the positional semantics.

This paper asks a narrower question than whether a model can extrapolate to a longer dense
context: can independently produced native K/V retain an auditable position identity, and what
errors remain after that identity is repaired? Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item a reusable position strategy supporting learned absolute, sinusoidal, and RoPE
semantics without changing the default absolute checkpoint layout;
\item a weight-preserving RoPE SelfAttention-to-PRA conversion whose no-memory logits match
the source model;
\item compact source-relative offsets, explicit position metadata, and pre/post-position state
labels on cached native K/V;
\item probes separating position reset, contextual block fragmentation, overlap, pre- versus
post-RoPE K, retrieved-distance policies, and continuously advancing implicit-head behavior; and
\item five-seed tiny/small evidence, including negative routing and variance results that
constrain the claims.
\end{itemize}

\section{Progressive Retrieval Attention in Brief}

PRA keeps a recent direct tail continuously visible and makes older or external material
addressable. A long source is first encoded in model-safe blocks. Each layer publishes its
native K/V, and those states are sliced into smaller routing chunks. A gist is a compact routing
summary of a chunk; it is \emph{not} the chunk's K/V and is never used as a substitute attention
value. At query time, gist scores select chunks, the selected chunks' full token-level native
K/V are materialized, and memory K/V attend beside direct K/V under one softmax.

Four lengths describe different constraints:
\begin{equation}
 L_{\rm logical} \gg L_{\rm model\_op} \ge L_{\rm encode} > L_{\rm route},
 \qquad
 L_{\rm direct}+L_{\rm materialized}+L_{\rm reserve}\le L_{\rm model\_op}.
 \label{eq:lengths}
\end{equation}
$L_{\rm logical}$ is the source coordinate span. $L_{\rm model\_op}$ is the maximum number of
tokens in one model invocation. $L_{\rm position}$, introduced below, is the largest
representable coordinate and need not equal the operation limit. $L_{\rm encode}$ bounds
contextual cache construction, while $L_{\rm route}$ controls retrieval granularity. A long
logical source therefore does not imply a long dense model call.

\subsection{A concrete position-reset failure}

Consider tokens A--H at logical positions 0--7, encoded as A--D and E--H. If the second block
restarts at zero, E receives the coordinate formerly assigned to A. Source-relative encoding
instead uses
\begin{equation}
 p_{\rm logical}(j)=p_{\rm chunk,start}+j.
 \label{eq:logical-position}
\end{equation}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrrrrr}
\toprule
Source token & A & B & C & D & E & F & G & H \\
Source position & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\
Reset chunk position & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\
Offset chunk position & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Thus token 37 within a block beginning at 4096 receives position 4133. A contiguous routing
chunk needs one integer start offset, not a position tensor per token; explicit tensors remain
useful for irregular layouts and audit output.

For a continuously advancing prompt head, rollover preserves one coordinate system as direct
tokens become historical memory. Independent URI references are different: each source owns
its own coordinate system unless an application defines a shared layout. The implementation
therefore treats source identity and source-relative offset as cache provenance rather than
silently concatenating unrelated documents into one global axis.

\section{Background and Related Work}

Learned absolute embeddings add a position-dependent vector before the decoder stack and
therefore entangle content and position in later hidden states. Transformer-XL introduced
relative positional treatment for recurrent segment memory \cite{dai2019transformerxl}.
Shortformer moved absolute position information to queries and keys to improve cached-state
reuse across subsequences \cite{press2021shortformer}. ALiBi adds distance-dependent attention
biases for train-short, test-long extrapolation \cite{press2022alibi}.

RoPE rotates query and key feature pairs by position-dependent phases, making their dot
product depend on relative displacement while each vector retains an absolute phase
\cite{su2021roformer}. Position Interpolation rescales indices into a trained range
\cite{chen2023positioninterpolation}; LongRoPE uses nonuniform interpolation and progressive
extension \cite{ding2024longrope}. Those methods enlarge dense model context. Our question is
different: how should positions behave when only selected externally stored native K/V is
returned? StreamingLLM preserves selected chronological cache states and attention sinks
\cite{xiao2023streamingllm}; PRA retrieves URI- or request-addressed chunks, so it must also
specify source positions, relocation, and routing semantics.

Cache reuse systems expose adjacent parts of the same problem. Prompt Cache reuses modular
attention states while preserving a position-aware prompt schema \cite{gim2023promptcache}.
CacheBlend observes that independently cached non-prefix chunks miss preceding context and
selectively recomputes states \cite{yao2024cacheblend}. MiniPIC stores unrotated keys and binds
RoPE positions inside attention \cite{ordonez2026minipic}; SemPIC instead learns a cache writer
to reduce residual context dependence \cite{xie2026sempic}. These approaches motivate the
same distinction tested here: correcting coordinates does not reconstruct absent causal
context, and flexible position binding does not by itself select useful memory.

\section{Positional Semantics for Native K/V}

\subsection{What RoPE invariance does and does not say}

For one head of width $d_h$, let $R_p$ be the block-diagonal rotation at position $p$:
\begin{equation}
  \widetilde q_i = R_i q_i, \qquad \widetilde k_j = R_j k_j.
\end{equation}
Orthogonality and the group property give
\begin{equation}
  \widetilde q_i^\top \widetilde k_j
  = q_i^\top R_i^\top R_j k_j
  = q_i^\top R_{j-i} k_j.
\end{equation}
Thus translating both positions by $c$ leaves the score unchanged:
$(R_{i+c}q_i)^\top(R_{j+c}k_j)=q_i^\top R_{j-i}k_j$. This supports relocation of a complete
query--memory relation. It does not imply that moving only a cached key leaves attention
unchanged; a fixed query should observe the changed relative distance.

\subsection{Three independent choices}

Three choices are often collapsed into "RoPE support":
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Encoding positions}: local block indices or logical source indices.
\item \textbf{Storage state}: pre-positional $K_{\rm raw}$ plus metadata, or published
post-RoPE $K_{\rm rope}$.
\item \textbf{Materialization policy}: preserve source distance, reset locally, relocate as a
block, compress distance, or retain order only.
\end{enumerate}
The canonical implementation publishes post-position K. Pre-position storage remains
experimental because deferred rotation changes the cache representation and runtime work.

\subsection{Position and contextualization fragmentation}

If a dense source is split into bounded blocks, local position reset changes absolute
embeddings and post-RoPE key phases. Independently, later blocks lose causal left context,
which changes hidden states even with perfect position IDs. We call these \emph{positional
fragmentation} and \emph{contextualization fragmentation}. Logical global positions can
remove the first at layer 0. Encoding overlap can only approximate the second.

\section{Implementation}

The shared code adds a registry-backed \texttt{PositionEncoding} interface. Its first method
positions token embeddings; its second transforms per-head Q/K with shape $[B,H,T,d_h]$.
Absolute mode adds the existing learned table and leaves projected Q/K unchanged. RoPE mode
leaves token embeddings unchanged and rotates Q/K. A position-aware SelfAttention block is
instantiated only for non-absolute modes, so old absolute models retain their original
\texttt{TransformerEncoderLayer} state names and behavior.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Position mechanisms under retrieved native-K/V reuse.}
\label{tab:position-mechanisms}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lXXX}
\toprule
Mechanism & Source offsets & Coordinate range & Retrieval-time rebinding \\
\midrule
Learned absolute & Added before layer 0 & Limited by learned table $L_{\rm position}$ & Not separable from hidden state \\
Sinusoidal absolute & Added before layer 0 & Formula-defined; extrapolation unverified & Not separable from hidden state \\
RoPE, post-position K & Applied to Q/K & Formula-defined; extrapolation unverified & No, without inverse rotation \\
RoPE, pre-position K & Applied when consumed & Formula-defined; extrapolation unverified & Yes, by choosing effective IDs \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The configuration separates \texttt{model\_max\_context\_tokens}, the native operation limit,
from \texttt{position\_capacity}. Learned absolute IDs are checked against the latter. This
allows, for example, a 32-token bounded operation to use positions 160--191 in a model with a
192-entry position table. RoPE accepts larger integer IDs, but acceptance by the formula is not
evidence that a model trained only on short distances will use them well.

PRA attention applies the same strategy in direct and cache-publication paths:
\begin{verbatim}
q = split_heads(q_proj(hidden))       # [B,H,T,Dh]
k = split_heads(k_proj(hidden))       # [B,H,T,Dh]
q, k = position.transform_qk(q, k, position_ids)

k_ref = split_heads(k_proj(reference_hidden))
_, k_ref = position.transform_qk(k_ref, k_ref, reference_positions)
cache.publish(k_ref, v_ref, position_ids, state="post_position")
\end{verbatim}
Values are not rotated. Each \texttt{LayerKV} records the positions used to publish K and a
\texttt{position\_state}. \texttt{ReferenceChunk} and \texttt{ReferenceChunkMemory} carry
\texttt{logical\_start} and \texttt{logical\_end}; validation, overlap, overflow splitting,
routing selection, CPU residency, and materialization preserve them. A contiguous chunk can
reconstruct token IDs as \texttt{logical\_start + arange(length)}. The result trace exposes the
same range so a consumer can audit which native states entered attention.

Conversion copies Q/K/V projections, output projection, norms, feed-forward layers,
embeddings, and language-model head from trained RoPE SelfAttention into PRA. With memory
disabled, source and converted logits match to unit-test tolerance. No memory adapter or
additional transport projection is introduced.

For a long prompt, the adapter keeps a direct tail and registers displaced tokens under
\texttt{\#\_\_head}. Bounded encoding produces native K/V, smaller chunks produce routing
gists, and whole chunks are selected under
\begin{equation}
 T_{\rm direct}+T_{\rm memory}+T_{\rm reserve}\leq L_{\rm model\_op}.
\end{equation}
RoPE can continue logical IDs past $L_{\rm model\_op}$ without enlarging an operation. Learned
absolute mode can do the same only while IDs remain below $L_{\rm position}$.

\section{Experimental Design}

\subsection{Models and training}

Controlled tiers are smaller than repository deployment profiles so all paired seeds fit one
NVIDIA GTX 950M. Both modes retain the learned position table, unused by RoPE, to match
parameter count exactly.

\centering

\begin{tabular}{lrrrrrr}
\toprule
Tier & Layers & Heads & $d$ & $d_{ff}$ & Parameters & Steps \\
\midrule
Tiny & 2 & 4 & 64 & 128 & 92,288 & 100 \\
Small & 4 & 4 & 128 & 256 & 580,608 & 150 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Seeds are $\{1,7,21,42,87\}$. Each absolute/RoPE pair uses the same 2,048 fixed-target
synthetic training examples, optimizer, batches, vocabulary, 192-token native context, and
training tokens. Every run reaches 100\% held-out answer-token accuracy. The downstream set
has 12 invariant source/query/answer examples; only source boundaries change. We evaluate
2, 5, 16, 32, and 64 splits.

PRA uses post-position K, local positions per independent reference, a 160-token whole-chunk
budget, and a four-token reserve in the split experiment. Conditions include dense full
context, tail only, native all, evidence oracle, gist-routed, shuffled, and memory-disabled.
Recovered context benefit is
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{RCB}=\frac{\mathcal L_{\rm tail}-\mathcal L_{\rm PRA}}
 {\mathcal L_{\rm tail}-\mathcal L_{\rm full}},
\end{equation}
reported without clipping.

\subsection{Additional probes}

The pure probe fixes Q/K/V across shifts up to 8,192 and measures logit RMSE, Jensen--Shannon
divergence, top-token agreement, and output RMSE. The pre/post probe compares stored
post-RoPE K with deferred rotation at identical positions, K-only relocation against a fixed
query, and common Q/K translation.

The raw-K policy probe holds Q/K/V content fixed while source--query distance grows from 1L to
32L, where $L=192$. Exact logical, immediately preceding local, 192-token clipped,
log-compressed, four-level bucketed, and remote-past assignments move each 32-token chunk as a
unit, preserving local order. This is a positional sensitivity probe, not downstream quality.
For systems cost, synchronized warm CUDA measurements separately time selected-key gather or
gather-plus-rotation, attention, and their combined path over 500 iterations for each of five
seeds.

The source-continuity decomposition compares dense 128-token encoding with 32-token blocks
under local reset, source-relative offsets, and 25\% or 50\% left overlap. It reports K RMSE by
layer and paired seed. The absolute table has 192 entries, so all logical IDs are representable
even though each encoding operation is bounded to 32 tokens.

The RoPE storage matrix crosses post- versus pre-position K with reset versus source-relative
coordinates: A is post/reset, B post/offset, C pre/reset, and D pre/offset. B is the reference.
A separate raw-K condition rebinds the source by $+512$. Runtime records gather, compact
position reconstruction, RoPE transform, materialization, attention, and the warm combined
path. All timings synchronize CUDA around 500 iterations and use five seeds.

The continuous-head probe uses $L_{\rm logical}=L_{\rm position}=192$ and
$L_{\rm model\_op}=32$. It keeps 8 direct tokens, encodes 16-token blocks, routes 8-token
chunks, and materializes at most 24 memory tokens. Four examples and five seeds progress from
reset routing to offset routing, offset plus 50\% overlap, and a constructed oracle containing
the first evidence chunk. This oracle tests one selection hypothesis; it is not ground-truth
proof that no other chunk helps. The earlier distance-scaling head probe is retained separately.

Before result inspection we recorded the following expected directions: offsets should reduce
reset error; overlap should reduce the remaining contextual error; matched pre/post storage
should agree; intentional K-only rebinding should change attention; and the constructed oracle
should be no worse than routed selection. We report failed and partial expectations rather than
discarding them.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Pure translation and storage}

Across head dimensions 8--64, common translation preserves the top-attended token exactly.
Maximum logit RMSE is $5.79\times10^{-5}$, maximum JS divergence is $3.00\times10^{-7}$,
and maximum output RMSE is $1.80\times10^{-5}$ (Figure~\ref{fig:translation}).
\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.72\linewidth]{../shared/results/paper1_5_rope/rope_translation.png}
\caption{Numerical RoPE common-translation invariance. The logarithmic vertical axis exposes
floating-point error, not a quality effect.}
\label{fig:translation}
\end{figure}

The trained-model storage matrix gives the same result at the model output
(Figure~\ref{fig:storage-matrix}). Post/reset A equals pre/reset C exactly; post/offset B equals
pre/offset D exactly. B and D reproduce the offset reference with zero output RMSE and top-token
agreement 1. Reset A/C instead have mean K RMSE 0.648, output RMSE 0.063, and top-token
agreement 0.225. Rebinding raw K by $+512$ gives output RMSE 0.093 and agreement 0.05. The
matrix rejects two possible misconceptions: raw K is not intrinsically more accurate when
effective IDs match, and changing K coordinates alone is not an invariant operation.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.88\linewidth]{../shared/results/paper1_5_rope/rope_storage_matrix.png}
\caption{Four-cell RoPE storage matrix over both tiers, all layers, and five seeds. Deferred
rotation is exact at matched effective positions; reset coordinates and intentional rebinding
produce the expected attention changes.}
\label{fig:storage-matrix}

The earlier tensor probe agrees: moving K alone by 128--8,192 positions changes fixed-query
scores, while translating Q and K together remains near invariant (maximum RMSE
$4.91\times10^{-4}$ at 8,192). Storage state therefore controls when positions can be bound,
not whether the same binding is accurate.

\subsection{Deferred rotation and distance precision}

Exact logical assignment reproduces post-position K, scores, and outputs. The approximate
policies are not numerically neutral. At 32L in the small tensor tier, their mean output RMSE
against exact assignment spans 0.289--0.326 and top-token agreement is 0--0.05. Tiny-tier
output RMSE spans 0.259--0.290 and agreement is 0.05--0.15. These are five-seed random-content
attention probes: they show geometric sensitivity, not task-loss degradation. A compressed
policy therefore requires training or adaptation evidence before deployment.

\subsection{Source-relative continuity and overlap}

Source-relative offsets make layer-0 K exactly match the dense 128-token comparator in every
tier, positional family, and seed. Reset layer-0 K RMSE ranges from 0.559 to 0.667 by
tier--position mean. The exact repair under both learned absolute embeddings and RoPE shows
that source coordinate identity, rather than RoPE specifically, is the necessary first step.

Deeper layers retain contextualization fragmentation (Table~\ref{tab:offset-decomposition}).
Offsets lower final-layer RMSE in all five seeds for all four model conditions. Fifty-percent
overlap then lowers every tiny seed, lowers four of five small RoPE seeds, but lowers only two
of five small absolute seeds and slightly worsens that mean. It processes 1.375 times as many
tokens. Overlap is therefore a tunable historical-context approximation, not a guaranteed or
monotonic repair.

\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{What the position contract solves.} Source-relative offsets, not RoPE alone, restore
first-layer coordinate identity. Learned absolute positions do so within their table capacity;
RoPE decouples formula-defined coordinates from that table and provides common-translation
semantics. The continuous-head experiment confirms that a logical span can advance while every
native operation remains bounded.

\paragraph{What the contract does not solve.} It does not restore left context lost by
independent encoding, choose the relevant chunk, prevent harmful attention to irrelevant K/V,
or guarantee extrapolation from short training contexts. The small-absolute overlap reversal,
tiny-RoPE oracle reversal, small native-all reversal, and weak routed conditions are useful
counterexamples: position correctness is necessary for continuity but insufficient for useful
retrieval.

\paragraph{Post- versus pre-position K.} Post-position K is immediately compatible with
attention but binds the cache to source positions. Pre-position K is portable and compact
offset metadata is small, but deferred rotation adds materialization work and requires a
distance policy. Here it is 3.62 times the 160-token combined warm microbenchmark and does not
reduce cache bytes.
Exact logical assignment is faithful; compressed, bucketed, and order-preserving relocation
change attention enough that their task quality remains an empirical question.

\paragraph{Engineering implication.} Source identity, logical range, position state, and
positional mechanism belong in cache provenance even when K is post-position. Without them, a
consumer cannot audit local versus logical coordinates, reproduce a score, or safely introduce
deferred rotation. For contiguous chunks, one logical start is enough for execution; full IDs
can be reconstructed for traces.

\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}

These are educational controlled models, not pretrained LLMs. The character tokenizer,
binary task, 12 split examples, four head examples, and GTX 950M limit external validity.
Five seeds permit paired direction checks but weak exact significance; an all-same-direction
effect still has two-sided $p=0.0625$. Mean loss is sensitive to rare catastrophes. The older
split benchmark uses independent references with effective local positions and should not be
read as the continuous-head source-offset result. The constructed oracle assumes the first
evidence chunk and can omit useful context; it is a diagnostic intervention, not known optimal
selection. Pre-position storage is not the canonical runtime path. We do not report production
throughput, large-model memory savings, natural-language QA, or
unrestricted streaming quality. Streaming tests establish bounded rollover mechanics for
absolute and RoPE modes, not useful generation beyond the native horizon. The distance-policy
probe uses random content and must not be read as a language-model accuracy comparison.

\section{Conclusion}

Retrieved native-KV memory needs an explicit positional contract. A compact source offset
removes first-layer reset error for learned absolute positions and RoPE alike. RoPE additionally
supports deferred binding and clean common translation, but the unfused raw-K path is slower
and K-only displacement deliberately changes attention. Deeper representations still depend on
causal encoding context, and end-task quality still depends on selection and the composition of
retrieved chunks. The next test is therefore not merely a longer coordinate range: it is
pretrained-model evaluation that measures position identity, contextual recomputation, routing
recall, and answer quality separately.
