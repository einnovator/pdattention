## Evidence boundary

The principal scale artifact retains seed-level aggregates, not the per-question rows needed for target-hit/miss stratification. The manuscript therefore limits those results to controlled answer-code transport and makes the causal rebuild a new experiment rather than presenting redundant-evidence or answer-propagation explanations as established.
