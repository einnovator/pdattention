# Audit and alerts

Audit and Alerts are central workspace views selected from the Activity section in the left pane.

## Audit log

The audit log records governed changes and engine actions. Entries identify time, action, target, reason, and result. Use it to verify that an operation was accepted and to reconstruct who changed fleet intent.

## Alerts

Alerts aggregate current engine conditions such as drift, offline state, lifecycle pressure, or adapter-reported warnings. Open the affected engine for detailed status and telemetry before taking action.

Refresh either view from its workspace toolbar. The global refresh button updates fleet state and the currently open activity view.
